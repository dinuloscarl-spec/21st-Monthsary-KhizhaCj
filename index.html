Happy 21st monthsary. Twenty-one months, and I still get excited every time your name pops up on my screen.

First, thank you. Thank you for your understanding: for every time I was late, tired, buried in school and projects, or just not my best self, and you stayed patient anyway. Thank you for choosing to understand me instead of giving up on me.

Thank you for all your sacrifices too. The time, the effort, the little things you gave up and never made me feel guilty about. I see them. I may not say it enough, but I notice every single one, and I’m grateful for each.

And I’m so sorry. I’m sorry for all the mistakes I’ve made: the times I hurt you, the words I should have said better, the moments I should have listened more. You deserved better in those moments, and I’m working every day to be better for you.

You make me want to grow. Every mistake taught me how much you matter, and I’d rather keep learning with you than be perfect without you.

I love you sooooo much, loveyyy. Not just on our monthsary, but on every ordinary day too. Here’s to 21 months, and to every month after this one.

Forever yours,
CJ
